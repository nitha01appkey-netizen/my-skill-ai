# My AI Skills Collection

Welcome to my repository! This is a collection of AI skills and prompts that I have developed. These prompts are designed to help users discover, guide, and track various activities based on their specific needs.

## 🚀 About the Project
This project was given by my supervisor after I learned about Claude AI and the `SKILL.md` framework. I created these skills to help users learn and find the information they need easily. 

There are **6 unique skills** in this repository, each with its own special advantages designed to make everyday tasks much simpler for the user.

## 📂 Collection of Skills & Features

### 1. 🌴 `bali-tour-guide.skill`
An AI assistant designed to guide foreign tourists around Bali. If users want to go to a certain place or are unsure where to visit, this skill can provide personalized travel recommendations and give directions to those destinations.

### 2. 📝 `english-assignment-checker.skill`
A useful tool that helps students (both school and university level) to check, review, and refine their English assignments.

### 3. 🍱 `food-and-drink-mood-tracker.skill`
A skill that helps users log their daily food and drink intake, alongside tracking their mood when consuming them. Additionally, it estimates general calorie counts and provides suggestions on what to eat or drink next if the daily calorie intake is too high or too low.

### 4. 📓 `personal-daily-report.skill`
A personal logging assistant that records everything the user does in a day. It can track daily to-do lists, moods, activities, and personal reflections for the day.

### 5. 🔬 `science-learning-support-for-high-school.skill`
A dedicated study companion for high school students. This skill helps users easily understand various science (IPA) subjects and materials.

### 6. 🗣️ `translator.skill`
An AI tool used to refine English-to-Indonesian and Indonesian-to-English translations. It ensures that the translated text feels natural, context-aware, and not stiff or literal.
